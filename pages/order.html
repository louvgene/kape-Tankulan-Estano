How to Order

Ordering steps

  1. Look in menu and choose your drink or snack.
  2. Tell the counter personnel your order at the counter.
  3. Pay in cash or by e-wallet..
  4. Wait for your name to be called, then enjoy!

Opening hours: Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed on Sundays.
